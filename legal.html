Privacy, in plain words

Pairlum is a private space for two people. What you share — moments, notes, letters, photos, voice notes — is stored on Pairlum's servers so it reaches both your devices, and only the two of you can open it. It is never sold and never used for advertising.

Your recordings are written up only if you switch that on for yourself (Settings → Privacy). Then your own voice notes and clips are sent to be transcribed and summarised; the words are kept, the sound is not. Your partner decides separately for theirs.

Removing something keeps it in Recently Deleted for 30 days, in case you change your mind, and then clears it for good. Deleting your account removes your login and everything you added; what your partner added stays with them. You can export a full copy of your space at any time.

On the pairlum.com website, Google Analytics stores cookies only if you choose “Allow”, and you can change your mind at any time (see Cookies & storage).

Privacy policy

Who we are: Pairlum is operated by Om Dangi ([company name — to be added], [registered address — to be added], India). For anything about your data, write to pairlum.co@gmail.com. This policy is written to meet India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (the "DPDP Act"); in its words, we are the Data Fiduciary for your personal data and you are the Data Principal.

What we collect, and why:

  • Your email address and password (the password stored only as a secure hash), to give you an account and keep it yours.
  • What you and your partner add to your space — moments, notes, letters, chapters, plans, answers, profile details, photos, clips and voice notes — to keep your space and bring it to both your devices.
  • If you switch on write-ups: the words of your own recordings, with a short title, a feeling and the topics, so a recording can be found again later.
  • A short log of live listening (who listened, when and for how long). The sound itself is sent directly between your two devices and is never recorded or stored.
  • Product counts with no content in them: which of a small, fixed list of things happened (for example "a space was created" or "the membership page was opened"), by whom, in which space and when — never what anyone wrote, said or shared. They help us understand whether Pairlum is working.
  • If you choose to import from Google Photos, only the photos you pick.
  • On the pairlum.com website, only if you choose “Allow”: which pages were viewed and which buttons were used (for example, opening Pairlum), counted by Google Analytics with a random identifier. Google Analytics never receives your name, your email or anything in your space. See Cookies & storage.
  • If you joined the early-access list before launch: the first name, email address and optional relationship category you entered, with the date, to send the launch updates you asked for. New early-access signups have closed; we delete these details when you ask.

Consent: by creating an account you agree to Pairlum handling the data above for these purposes. Optional features — write-ups, Google Photos import, live listening — each ask you first, and you can switch them off at any time. Switching something off stops it from then on.

Who can see it: only the two people in a space can see what is in it. We don't look at your space's contents. The people who run Pairlum can technically reach the database; we do so only to fix a problem you ask us to look at, to investigate abuse or a security issue, or when the law requires it.

  • Google Analytics — counting visits to the website, only if you choose “Allow”. It is not used for advertising.

Pairlum is not end-to-end encrypted: our servers can read what is stored so they can bring it to both your devices.

How long we keep it: what you add is kept while your account exists. Something you remove waits in Recently Deleted for 30 days and is then cleared for good, with its photo. When you delete your account, your login, your profile and everything you added — with your photos, clips, recordings and their write-ups — are deleted, and your pairing ends for both of you. What your partner added stays with them, and so do things you shared that belong to both of you, such as chapters. Product counts are kept but no longer linked to you. Copies inside our providers' backups are removed on their own schedule. [backup retention period — to be confirmed]

Security: your space is protected by access rules on our servers: only its two members can read it, and each person can change only what is theirs. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal data, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board as the DPDP Act requires.

Age: Pairlum is only for adults 18 and over. We don't knowingly hold data about anyone younger; if you believe we do, write to us and we'll delete it.
