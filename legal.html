Privacy, in plain words

Pairlum is built for two people. Everything you share — moments, notes, letters, recordings — lives in your shared space and is visible only to the two of you, never to other members, and never sold to advertisers.

A voice recording stays on your device unless you choose to share it into your space. If Pairlum ever adds a feature that reads a recording to write it up (so you can search it later by meaning), that only happens once such a service exists, nothing leaves your device before then, and we'll tell you plainly when it changes.

You can export your data or delete your space at any time from Settings. Deleting your space removes both partners' copies.

Privacy policy

This policy covers the Pairlum website and app (together, "Pairlum"), operated for couples who want a private, shared space.

What we collect: the email address you sign up with; the content you and your partner create inside your shared space (text, photos, voice recordings, letters); basic device/browser information needed to run the app; and, if you choose to connect it, data from Google Photos for importing memories.

How we use it: solely to operate your shared space, keep it in sync between your two devices, process membership payments, and respond to support requests. We do not sell personal data or use it for third-party advertising.

Who can see it: only the two members of a space. Pairlum staff do not read the contents of a space except where necessary to investigate abuse, a security issue, or at your explicit request for support.

Storage and security: content is stored using industry-standard encryption in transit and access controls at rest. No system is perfectly secure; we disclose any breach that could affect your data as required by law.

Your choices: you can export your data, delete individual items, or delete your whole space at any time. Deleting a space is permanent and removes it for both partners.

Retention: content is kept for as long as your space exists. If a membership lapses, we keep data for a reasonable grace period before deletion, so you can renew without losing your history.

Children: Pairlum is for adults 18 and over, in keeping with its purpose as a space for romantic partners.

Changes: if this policy changes materially, we'll notify members by email before the change takes effect.

Contact: pairlum.co@gmail.com.

Terms of service

By creating a Pairlum account you agree to these terms. If you disagree with them, please don't use Pairlum.

Eligibility: you must be 18 or older. A space is meant for two partners; each partner needs their own account.

Your content: you own what you and your partner create in your space. You're responsible for having the right to share anything you upload, and for respecting your partner's consent when sharing moments that involve them.

Acceptable use: don't use Pairlum to harass, surveil without consent, or harm your partner or anyone else. Spaces found in violation may be suspended.

Membership and billing: paid plans renew yearly unless cancelled. Prices are shown on the Membership page and will never change for existing content without notice.

The Pairlum Book: a printed Book becomes available after a full year on the Book membership, one per couple space, as described on the Membership page. Shipping times and print specifications will be confirmed before the first print run.

Termination: you may delete your space at any time. We may suspend a space that violates these terms or the law, after a good-faith attempt to reach you where possible.

No warranty: Pairlum is provided "as is" during this prototype/early-access period; features may change as the product develops.

Governing law: these terms are governed by the laws of India, without regard to conflict-of-law rules.

Refund policy

If something isn't working as described, email pairlum.co@gmail.com within 14 days of your payment and we'll make it right, including a full refund if appropriate.

After 14 days, yearly membership fees are non-refundable for the remainder of that year, but you can cancel auto-renewal at any time so you're not charged again.

If a Book membership is cancelled before a Book has been printed, the Book-specific portion of your most recent payment is refunded; the Digital membership portion is handled under the policy above.
