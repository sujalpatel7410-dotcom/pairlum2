## 1. Scope and privacy principle

Pairlum is a private relationship space. This Privacy Policy applies to pairlum.com, Pairlum accounts and Couple Spaces, Memories, Letters, Chapters, Time Capsules, Parallel Moments, reactions, Pairlum Book, free public tools, payments, support and other Pairlum services that link to it.

Pairlum is designed around four permanent principles: private relationship content is private by default; users retain ownership of what they create; private memories are not sold as an advertising product; and private relationship content is not used to train AI models by default.

## 2. Adults only

Pairlum V1 is intended only for people aged 18 years or older. By creating an account, a user confirms that they are at least 18. Pairlum may restrict or delete an ineligible minor account when identified, subject to applicable law.

## 3. Account and Couple Space information

- Name, email address, authentication/account identifiers, account settings and security/session information.

- Partner invitation information, Couple Space identifiers, optional relationship start/anniversary dates, milestones, covers and other information deliberately added by the Couple.

- Pairlum should not require unnecessary sensitive information simply for personalization.

## 4. Private Content

"Private Content" includes photographs, videos, voice recordings, notes, captions, Memories, Letters, Open When/Locked Letters, Time Capsules, Chapters, Parallel Moments, reactions, Book selections/layouts and other content intentionally added to a Couple Space.

Private Content is not public by default. A connected partner may access content according to the rules of the relevant feature. Joining Pairlum does not grant Pairlum marketing rights over that content.

## 5. Information your partner may provide

A connected partner may invite you, upload a photograph containing you, create a shared Memory, write a Letter, add a milestone, react, or contribute to a shared object. Pairlum must distinguish partner sharing from public disclosure and should provide appropriate product controls.

## 6. Technical, usage and analytics information

- Browser/device/OS, IP and network/security information, session identifiers, application version, referrer/campaign data, performance/error information and feature events.

- Analytics should measure behaviour around private content rather than the substance of the content. Example: `memory_created=true`, not the photograph or Letter body.

- Private session replay is disabled in authenticated Couple Spaces for V1.

## 7. Photo and media metadata

Original media may contain capture date, device details and location metadata. Pairlum may use limited metadata to organize a Memory, strip unnecessary metadata from viewing derivatives, and preserve the private original where required for archival/Book quality. Visible location should remain user-controlled where practical.

## 8. Payments

An external payment provider processes payment credentials. Pairlum may retain order/payment IDs, amount, currency, status, date, cohort, refund status and limited payment-method information. Pairlum does not intend to store full card numbers, CVV, UPI PINs or banking passwords.

## 9. Pairlum Book information

- Eligibility date, selected Memories, selected media, Book layout, cover, proof/approval status and print-quality status.

- Recipient name, shipping address, telephone number where courier-required, order/production/tracking status.

## 10. Support and feedback

Pairlum may process contact details, tickets, feedback, screenshots voluntarily supplied, and technical details relevant to a support issue. Founding feedback does not transfer ownership of private memories.

## 11. Purposes of processing

- Process memberships, payments/refunds, support, service communications and legally required records.

Users retain ownership of the content they lawfully create or upload. Pairlum receives only the limited, non-exclusive permission reasonably necessary to host, store, back up, transcode, generate previews, display to authorized users, transmit, create an approved Book and otherwise provide requested features.

The licence is not a general right to sell private content or use it in advertising.

## 13. AI and private memories

Pairlum may later offer optional AI-assisted organization, Book curation, duplicate detection, question generation or similar features. Private photographs, video, voice, Letters, Notes and Time Capsules are not a general AI-training dataset by default.

If a requested AI feature requires private content to be processed by an AI service, the relevant processing should be explained. Any broader AI training using private relationship content requires a separate appropriate permission process.

## 14. No sale of intimate relationship data

Pairlum does not sell Private Content or intimate relationship information to advertisers and should not build advertising profiles based on inferred relationship quality, conflicts, private Letters, photos or emotional vulnerability.

## 15. When information may be shared

- With the connected partner according to feature permissions.

- With service providers for authentication, cloud infrastructure/storage, media processing, payments, email, analytics/error monitoring, Book printing and logistics, limited to what the provider needs.

- For valid legal/safety/security reasons where disclosure or preservation is required or permitted.

- As part of a legitimate business reorganization subject to applicable safeguards.

- With separate user permission for testimonials, public stories or marketing.

## 16. Public profiles

Pairlum V1 does not normally provide public Couple profiles, follower lists, public Memory feeds, publicly searchable couples or public relationship scores.

## 17. Security safeguards

- Authenticated access, server-side authorization, private media storage, short-lived signed media access, encryption in transit and at rest where supported.

- Database access controls/RLS, administrator MFA, least privilege, audit logging, backups and restricted production access.

- Pairlum does not claim "100% security" and does not claim end-to-end encryption unless it is actually implemented.

## 18. Administrative access

Ordinary administration should not require casual access to Private Content. Admin tools should expose account, membership, storage, payment, Book and error metadata. Exceptional private-content access must have a legitimate purpose, minimum scope and audit trail as defined in D9.

## 19. Retention and deletion

Pairlum retains information only as long as reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose or legal obligation. Eligible ordinary deleted content is intended to remain in Recently Deleted for 30 days, after which permanent deletion from active systems begins.

Protected backups may retain deleted information temporarily until the backup lifecycle expires. Payment, accounting, fraud/security and legal records may require longer retention. Specific production retention periods must be finalized before publication.

## 20. Account vs Couple Space

An individual account and the shared Couple Space are separate. One partner deleting an individual account should not automatically and silently erase everything belonging to the other partner. Shared-content deletion must account for who contributed the content, applicable rights, legal obligations and the design of the relevant shared object.

## 21. Separation

Pairlum should permit users to leave a Couple Space, export eligible data, delete eligible content or delete an account without manipulative breakup/guilt mechanics. Founding status does not obligate a couple to remain together.

- Correct inaccurate/incomplete information.

- Delete eligible content or request account deletion.

- Withdraw consent where consent is the applicable basis.

- Use Pairlum's product export functionality for eligible data, subject to the rights of the other partner.

- Raise a privacy grievance and use applicable statutory remedies.

- Use nomination rights where and when applicable under Indian data-protection law.

Pairlum may use essential authentication/security/session technologies, analytics, and later optional marketing technologies. Marketing tracking is not to be disguised as essential. D6 gives the detailed notice.

## 24. Free public tools

Couple Questions, Date Ideas and Love Messages may process the inputs required to generate the requested result. Pairlum should avoid unnecessarily retaining intimate free-tool inputs. Saved results may become account data when the user deliberately saves them.

## 20. Additional copies/upgrades

Extra copies, larger formats, additional pages, alternative covers, premium packaging or expedited production may be separately priced. Declining an upgrade must not prevent a valid eligible Couple from receiving the standard included Book.

Pairlum should not impose a hidden short claim deadline. Any future claim period must be reasonable, disclosed and reminded. ## 23. Separation

If a Couple separates, Book production may require additional authorization/review because the draft can contain contributions from both people. Pairlum should protect privacy/content rights and avoid disclosing one former partner's private content without a proper basis.

Approved production files may be retained only as long as reasonably necessary for printing, quality, reprints, support/disputes and legal obligations. Printer/vendor confidentiality/security should be treated as part of vendor selection, not just print quality/cost.

## 25. Vendor failure and equivalent fulfilment

If the original printer/material becomes unavailable, Pairlum should seek an equivalent or better provider/specification or another appropriate remedy. An earned Book benefit should not vanish merely because one vendor closes.

# D8 — Data Export, Account & Deletion Policy

PUBLICATION DRAFT  |  Last updated: 26 August 2026  |  Target effective date: 4 September 2026

Operator: [LEGAL NAME / ENTITY OPERATING PAIRLUM], [LEGAL / BUSINESS ADDRESS], India. Replace all contact placeholders and planned domain emails before publication.

## 1. Core distinction

Pairlum distinguishes individual account from shared Couple Space. One partner leaving/deleting their account must not automatically and silently destroy everything belonging to the other partner, while the shared structure must not create an unlimited partner veto over valid privacy rights.

## 2. Four separate actions

- Delete an item — e.g., one Memory.

- Leave Couple Space — disconnect relationship access while potentially retaining the individual account.

- Delete My Account — delete the individual account/eligible personal data.

- Delete Our Couple Space — permanently close the shared archive. Each action has different consequences.

## 3. Access and correction

Users may request information concerning personal data Pairlum processes and may correct inaccurate/incomplete information through self-service controls or privacy support, subject to applicable law and identity verification.

## 4. Export My Data / Export Our Pairlum

Pairlum intends to provide useful product export functionality. An export may include eligible Photos, Videos, Voice, Memories, Notes, Letters, Chapters, Time Capsules and metadata, ideally using preserved originals where available.

Export is not a mechanism to reveal another partner's private draft/locked content or to override rights/access controls. Pairlum may structure/redact exports to protect both users.

## 5. Export security

Exports may require reauthentication, one-time/expiring links, secure delivery or encryption. Pairlum should not email an entire intimate archive as an unprotected attachment.

## 6. Delete one Memory

Eligible deleted content normally moves to Recently Deleted for 30 days before active-system permanent deletion. Authorized users may restore during the recovery period. "Delete Permanently" requires stronger confirmation.

## 7. Backups and lawful retention

Permanent deletion begins removal from active systems, but information may remain temporarily in protected backups and limited security/payment/legal records until the relevant lifecycle/obligation ends. Pairlum must not promise instant deletion from every backup or retain full private archives indefinitely "just in case."

## 8. Delete My Account

Before account deletion Pairlum should explain the effect on the Couple Space, user-created/shared content, export, Founding status, Book eligibility and records that must remain. Reauthentication is required. Partner B's account and Partner B-created content are not automatically erased by Partner A's deletion.

## 9. Shared content

Parallel Moments, shared Chapters, collaborative Capsules, paired Memories and Book projects may contain contributions from both users. Pairlum may need to remove one contribution, preserve another, disable/split the object or manually review. Do not implement the simplistic rule "uploader controls everything" or "partner keeps everything."

## 10. Leave Couple Space

Leaving is separate from account deletion. It can stop future relationship access/activity while preserving the individual account and enabling applicable export/deletion workflows. Pairlum does not require a breakup explanation or use guilt/streak mechanics.

## 11. Partner notification

Where appropriate and safe, Pairlum may notify the connected partner that the Pairlum connection ended without disclosing unnecessary reasons. Safety/security investigations may justify delaying or limiting notice.

## 12. Delete Our Couple Space

Entire Couple Space deletion is a higher-risk action. For an ordinary active Couple Space with both-partner contributions, the preferred V1 product rule is both-partner confirmation, where legally/operationally appropriate, plus strong warning, export-first option and reauthentication.

Two-partner confirmation is not absolute; abuse, death/incapacity, legal orders, abandoned/compromised accounts, unlawful content or individual statutory rights may require another reviewed process.

## 13. Couple Space deletion grace period

Pairlum should initially use a 30-day Scheduled for Deletion period for ordinary full-space deletion, unless applicable law/safety/unlawful content requires another timeline. Where allowed, pending deletion may be cancelled before final destruction.

## 14. Book and deletion

Permanent deletion before an unearned Book eligibility date normally ends the future benefit, and Pairlum must warn before deletion. If the Book has already been earned, Pairlum should clearly present options (complete Book, export, waive, other lawful route) rather than silently delete the earned entitlement. Approved Books already in production may require limited fulfilment retention.

## 15. Membership/refund are separate

Cancelling membership, receiving a refund, Founding withdrawal or account suspension are not themselves deletion requests. Data actions follow this policy.

## 16. Death/incapacity and nomination

Pairlum may develop a verified process for a duly nominated/authorized person to exercise applicable rights after death/incapacity. Pairlum must not release an archive simply because someone claims to have been the partner.

## 17. Identity verification

Rights/export/deletion requests may require authenticated-account verification, password re-entry, email confirmation, MFA or another proportionate step. Pairlum should not routinely demand excessive government documents if safer account verification is sufficient.

## 18. Admin request workflow

Admin states should include RECEIVED, IDENTITY_CHECK, IN_REVIEW, WAITING_FOR_USER, APPROVED, PROCESSING, COMPLETED and DECLINED. Log request ID, type, account/Couple, dates, verification, decision, administrator and reason without putting private content into the audit log.

## 19. Service providers and analytics

When eligible personal data is permanently deleted and no lawful retention purpose remains, Pairlum should cause relevant processors to delete according to contracts/technical processes. Analytics-linked data is handled where applicable, while private content should not have been sent to analytics in the first place.

## 20. Post-membership storage

Pairlum must define the exact post-expiry storage/access policy before paid memberships begin. Do not promise indefinite free cloud hosting, but do not implement sudden deletion merely because a membership expires. Provide clear notice and reasonable export/account-management options.

## 21. No data hostage / external copies

Pairlum should not use "pay forever or your relationship disappears immediately" as a retention model. Deleting Pairlum's copy also cannot delete independent copies another person already has outside Pairlum.

## 22. Export before deletion and no dark patterns

Before destructive account/Couple actions, prominently offer Export First where appropriate, explain the consequences, reauthenticate and confirm. Do not hide deletion, require sales calls, guilt users or force a public breakup reason.

# D9 — Internal Privacy & Administrative Access Policy

## 1. Core internal rule

Being technically able to access Private Content does not mean a founder, developer or operator is allowed to access it. Private Couple data may be accessed only for a legitimate authorized purpose, to the minimum extent required, through an appropriate role, with strong authentication and an audit record.

## 2. No curiosity access

No founder or team member may browse a Couple's photos, Letters, videos, Book or Time Capsules out of curiosity, because they know the users, or to find "interesting" content. Curiosity access is a serious internal privacy violation.

## 3. Data sensitivity levels

- Level 1 — Public: public website/articles/tools/pricing.

- Level 2 — Operational: Couple ID, membership/cohort, status, storage, payments, Book/support state.

- Level 3 — Personal: names, emails, address, security/account history.

- Level 4 — Private Relationship Content: photos, video, voice, Notes, captions, Letters, Time Capsules, private Book pages and relationship content. Level 4 has the strongest restrictions.

## 4. Current three-founder administrative hierarchy

Pairlum currently operates with three Co-Founders. Product/business titles do not override least-privilege rules.

## 5. Default admin view

The normal Admin Panel should show Couple ID, partner activation, membership/cohort, storage, payment, upload-processing, Book, support and technical status. It should not show a private gallery, Letter bodies, voice transcripts, full videos, Time Capsule contents or complete Book spreads by default.

## 6. Least privilege and deny by default

Every internal role receives only the permissions it needs. Server-side authorization is mandatory; hiding a button is not security. Access is denied unless explicitly allowed.

## 7. CEO / business administrator

The CEO may manage business overview, membership/cohorts, legal/policies, pricing, Founding, authorized refunds, support governance, Book liability and admin roles. CEO status is not permission to casually browse Private Content.

## 8. CTO / technical administrator

The CTO handles code, architecture, staging, deployment, databases, storage, security and technical logs. Debug using synthetic/staging data and metadata/error codes first. Production Level-4 access is exceptional, scoped, time-limited, reasoned and logged.

## 9. COO / operations administrator

The COO handles support operations, vendor coordination, Book fulfilment, customer operations and broader non-technical execution. The COO receives only the customer/order/support information necessary for the task; private archive access is exceptional and logged.

## 10. Future roles

As Pairlum grows, split SUPPORT, BOOK_OPERATIONS, FINANCE and SECURITY roles. Finance should not see Memories; Book Operations sees only approved production content; Support sees operational metadata and customer-provided evidence, not the entire archive.

## 11. MFA and individual accounts

MFA is mandatory for all three founders on production/admin access. Never share one admin account/password. Every action must be attributable to an individual identity.

## 12. Reauthentication for high-risk actions

- Level-4 content access; refunds; manual email/account changes; partner removal/disconnection; full export; permanent account/Couple deletion; admin role changes; break-glass access; sensitive security settings.

## 13. Two-person approval for critical actions

Because Pairlum now has a three-founder team, critical actions should use two-person approval where technically practical rather than relying on a "sole-founder exception." Recommended examples: changing another founder's admin role, disabling audit/security controls, broad production-data export, emergency private-content access without customer authorization, destructive bulk actions and rotation/transfer of master production ownership.

Routine authorized operations should not become bureaucratic; the purpose is to protect high-impact actions.

## 14. Support content access

If ordinary support genuinely needs to inspect a private Memory, ask the user for specific temporary authorization where practical. Customer-supplied screenshots/content are permission-limited to resolving the support case and do not become marketing/research/AI material.

## 15. Exceptions without advance customer permission

Advance permission may not be practical for security incidents, cross-couple exposure, NCII/child-safety reports, fraud, legal process or other urgent lawful needs. Access is still minimized, authorized and audited.

## 16. Book operations

Book operators receive the approved print file, order ID and fulfilment information only. Access to approved Book content is permitted for prepress/quality/reprint investigation; unrelated Pairlum Memories remain out of scope.

## 17. Private-content access audit event

`PRIVATE_CONTENT_ACCESSED` is a first-class audit event. Record administrator, role, Couple/resource ID, reason, ticket/incident, access type, timestamp/duration and outcome. Do not put the private content itself into the audit record.

## 18. Standard access reasons

- CUSTOMER_AUTHORIZED_SUPPORT

- SECURITY_INVESTIGATION

- PRIVACY_INCIDENT

- BOOK_PRODUCTION

- BOOK_REPRINT

- LEGAL_REQUEST

- DATA_RECOVERY

- ABUSE_INVESTIGATION

- OTHER_APPROVED (requires explanation)

## 19. Audit events

- ADMIN_LOGIN / LOGIN_FAILED; ADMIN_ROLE_GRANTED/CHANGED/REMOVED; PRIVATE_CONTENT_ACCESSED; CUSTOMER_EXPORT_GENERATED/DOWNLOADED; REFUND_INITIATED; CUSTOMER_EMAIL_CHANGED; COUPLE_DISCONNECTED; ACCOUNT/COUPLE_DELETION_APPROVED; FOUNDING_STATUS_REVOKED; BOOK_PRODUCTION_FILE_ACCESSED; BREAK_GLASS_ACTIVATED; SECURITY_SETTING_CHANGED.

## 20. Audit-log protection

Audit logs must be protected from unauthorized reading, modification and deletion. A person being audited must not be able to quietly erase the evidence. Log access itself should be restricted and monitored. Do not log passwords, OTPs, tokens, signed URLs, Letter bodies, photos, voice transcripts or secrets.

## 21. Break-glass access

Extraordinary access for a major breach, system-wide corruption, urgent legal/safety matter or recovery event must require strong authentication, explicit reason, narrow target, automatic audit, alert to another founder where feasible, and post-event review. It must never become a convenience shortcut.

## 22. Development and staging

Normal development uses fabricated/test data, licensed demo media and test payments. Do not copy real customer content into staging because it is convenient. CSS/frontend tasks do not justify production database access.

## 23. AI/coding tools

No founder/developer should paste real customer Letters, photos, videos, voice, Time Capsules or other Private Content into ChatGPT, Claude, coding assistants, public issue trackers or unrelated AI services by default. Reproduce bugs using synthetic strings/content whenever possible.

## 24. No internal AI training/research dataset

No one may build a relationship research/AI dataset from customer archives without a separately approved lawful governance process. Ordinary Terms acceptance is not blanket research consent.

## 25. No marketing/demo use

Never use a real Couple Space in investor/influencer/employee demos or copy "interesting" customer content into social media, decks or portfolios. Use synthetic demo accounts unless separate marketing permission specifically covers the material.

## 26. Production secrets

Supabase service credentials, database passwords, R2 secrets, payment/email keys, signing keys and admin secrets must not be committed to source, shared in WhatsApp/screenshots/public repos or given to people who do not need them. Dev/staging/production credentials must be separate.

## 27. Direct database access

Direct production DB access should be rare and use least-privilege roles, secure connections, explicit purpose and logging. Prefer admin/application-level tooling over superuser credentials.

## 28. Personal/local copies

Do not download customer archives to personal laptops/phones/USB/WhatsApp/personal Drive as a normal workflow. If a temporary local copy is genuinely required, use an approved secure device/location, limit scope, document purpose and delete the working copy afterward.

## 29. Screenshots and screen sharing

Do not screenshot private customer content for convenience or paste it into group chats. Use IDs, redacted/synthetic reproductions and sanitized logs. Avoid exposing customer information during screen shares/recorded meetings.

## 30. Vendor access and contracts

Printers, couriers, payment providers, analytics and other processors receive only the data necessary for their role. Processor contracts should address confidentiality, security, permitted purpose, access, retention/deletion, breach handling and subprocessors as appropriate.

## 31. High-risk customer operations

Manual email changes, partner removal, refunds, full exports and permanent deletions require appropriate identity/authorization checks and audit records. Never change a user email simply because their partner asks.

## 32. Incident response

Cross-couple exposure, wrong customer Book, public storage bucket, credential leak, wrong export, unauthorized admin access or destructive data incident are security/privacy incidents—not ordinary support tickets. Contain, investigate, document, notify where required, remediate and review.

## 33. Internal severity

- P0: cross-Couple data exposure, major credential compromise, destructive data loss, wrong-customer private Book.

- P1: significant security/access failure with limited scope.

- P2: weakness without confirmed significant exposure.

- P3: minor hardening issue.

## 34. Confidentiality and offboarding

Anyone with production/customer access must be bound by appropriate confidentiality obligations. When a founder/employee/contractor no longer requires access, promptly revoke admin/database/hosting/storage/payment/email/Git/security permissions and rotate affected secrets where necessary.

## 35. Access review

With the three-founder setup, perform a formal access review at least quarterly once production is live: who has access, role, production need, active keys, old contractors and privilege changes. Review immediately after any personnel/role change.

## 36. Device security

Founder/admin devices should use full-device encryption, strong login, current patches, automatic screen locking and MFA. Avoid sensitive administration from uncontrolled public/shared computers. Move toward managed work devices as Pairlum grows.

## 37. Analytics access and session replay

Analytics admins follow the same minimization rules. Use aggregate/pseudonymous events, not a second hidden database of Private Content. Session replay remains OFF inside authenticated Pairlum V1 and cannot be enabled unilaterally without privacy/technical approval and updated disclosure/consent where needed.

## 38. Relationship scoring prohibition

Internal staff must not create labels such as BAD_RELATIONSHIP, TOXIC_COUPLE, LIKELY_BREAKUP or LOVE_SCORE based on private behaviour/content. Pairlum is not a surveillance model of relationship quality.

## 39. Founder responsibility

Each Co-Founder is bound by this policy. Founder status does not exempt anyone from privacy rules. The CEO owns overall policy governance; the CTO owns technical enforcement/security implementation; the COO owns operational adherence across support/Book/vendor workflows. Major violations are escalated across the founders as appropriate.

## 40. Internal violations

Unauthorized private-data access may result in immediate access removal, investigation, termination of contractor/working access, evidence preservation, legal action and required notifications. No one may delete/alter audit evidence to conceal improper access.

## 41. Internal privacy test

Before touching customer data, ask: Why am I accessing this? Do I need this specific data? Can I solve it using metadata/synthetic data? Is access authorized? Will it be logged? Am I viewing only the minimum required? If those questions cannot be answered, do not access it.
