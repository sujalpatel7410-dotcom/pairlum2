A little clarity

Before your
First Chapter.

Everything you might want to know before joining us.

Can we use Pairlum today?

Yes. Pairlum is open. Open Pairlum, create your account and invite your partner. It runs in your browser on phones and computers, so there is nothing to download.

Who is Pairlum for?

Pairlum is made for adult couples, especially those living apart. Couples in the same city are welcome too, and you do not need to be married.

How do we get started?

Open Pairlum and create your account. Then send your partner an invite. Once they accept, the two of you share one private space.

Does my partner need their own account?

Yes. Each partner has their own account, and you both join the same private space through an invite.

Is Pairlum free?

Yes. Pairlum is free at launch. The free First Chapter has limited storage. Paid membership, with fuller access and more storage, is coming later, and you will see every detail before anything is charged.

Do we need two paid memberships?

No. When paid membership opens, one membership covers both partners in one shared space. Each person keeps their own account.

Does monthly membership include the physical Book?

No. Monthly membership is digital-only. A printed Pairlum Book comes only with Membership + Book, after one year on that membership.

What makes Pairlum different from our everyday chats?

Pairlum brings the small things from both your days into one lasting story: photos with your voice attached, parallel moments, letters and shared history. It is about everyday presence and memories you can return to.

Who can see our memories?

Your space is made for the two of you, not a public feed. How Pairlum stores and protects what you add is explained in the privacy notice ↗.

I joined the early-access list. What now?

Thank you for being early. You can start now: open Pairlum and create your account. We keep your early-access details only to send launch updates, and you can ask us to delete them at any time.

Can I delete my account or my old signup?

Yes. Email pairlum.co@gmail.com from the address you used to ask us to delete your account or your early-access signup. Contact us ↗.

Is there a refund if we change our minds?

Pairlum is free right now. When paid membership opens, you can ask for a full refund of your first payment within 7 days of paying, for any reason. Email pairlum.co@gmail.com from the address you paid with.
